# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive
feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the
community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention,
trolling, insults, threats, publishing private information, or conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify and enforce standards and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions or ban participation when behavior is inappropriate.
Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact methods on the
GitHub repository. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
